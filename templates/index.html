Research integrity note
All property listings, scores, graph edges and sample metrics in this standalone prototype are simulated for UI demonstration. They are not real listings or experimental results.
Illustrative score structure
Final score = normalised active contribution from direct preference matching + comparable-neighbour support (+ optional valid market-context evidence).

Unavailable components are excluded and active weights are renormalised in the documented method. The UI does not claim that the method outperforms baselines.
